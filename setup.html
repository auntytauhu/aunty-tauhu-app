App setup (one time only)

1Create the database tables

  1. Tap Copy database code below.
  2. Open supabase.com/dashboard, open your project (or tap New project, name it aunty-tauhu, choose region Singapore).
  3. Tap SQL Editor in the menu, then New query.
  4. Paste, then tap Run. You should see Aunty Tauhu database is ready.

2Turn off email confirmation

  1. In Supabase tap Authentication, then Sign In / Providers (or Providers), then Email.
  2. Turn off “Confirm email” and tap Save.
This lets staff log in straight after creating an account. They still can't see anything until you approve them in the app.

3Connect the app

  1. In Supabase tap Project Settings (gear), then API (or Data API and API Keys).
  2. Copy the Project URL and the anon public key and send them to Claude in the chat.
Never share the service_role / secret key.

4Create your owner account first

  1. Open the app: this link.
  2. Tap First time? Create an account, enter your name, email and a password.
  3. The first account becomes the owner automatically.

5Add it to your Home Screen

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

6Add your staff

  1. Send staff the app link. They open it, tap First time? Create an account and add it to their Home Screen.
  2. You open Settings → Staff and tap Approve. They only see customer orders.
